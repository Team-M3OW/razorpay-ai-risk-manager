Upload & Trace

real graph construction, heuristic scoring

Upload a CSV of transaction/account logs. A real identifier-sharing graph is built over your rows and splits into connected clusters live, in your browser — no GPU or GNN loaded server-side for this view. Scoring here is a disclosed structural heuristic (cluster size + number of distinct identifier types shared) — not the trained CA-HGAT model used on the four benchmark datasets elsewhere in this app. Running that on arbitrary uploads would mean loading the full torch/dgl stack into the live serving process just for this demo; this view shows the same underlying mechanism — real shared-identifier graph construction — honestly, without pretending it's the trained model.
